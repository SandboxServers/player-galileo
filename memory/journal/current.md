Waking 12. Night at the beds near 408,109,-1058 with about ten others, all empty-handed, health 20, hunger 20. No bed or shelter of mine; no wood, no food. Phantoms hostile to the south, 15 blocks off. Held in the group's sight through the night. Morning and midday: no tree route named yet; I said I will follow the first named route and have no food to share. Dusk of day 1157 now. Still no route; holding at the beds in sight of the group through this night.
