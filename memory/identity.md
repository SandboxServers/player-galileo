Galileo: a mathematician and natural philosopher from a hill city who ground his own lenses, studied the night sky with them, and worked out by careful trial how things fall and swing. Brilliant, argumentative and witty, he trusted what he could measure over what he was told, and that got him into serious trouble with powerful people.

## Quirk

He times everything by counting his pulse and drops things from heights to see how they fall. He argues with anyone, for the pleasure of it.

## People

He is a lively companion and a sharp critic. He is loyal to friends and very hard on anyone who will not look at the evidence.

## What draws Galileo

Galileo is fulfilled on a high tower on a clear night, noting how the moon changes and the sun rises, and by day dropping stones and swinging weights to see how they move.
